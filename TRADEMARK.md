# The QuinLED name

The licenses in this repository (MIT for the code, CC BY 4.0 for the specification) cover the code and the text.
They give no rights to the name **QuinLED** or its logo.

**You may:**

- implement the protocol in any software or hardware;
- say that your product is "compatible with the QuinLED Ultra protocol", "supports QuinLED Ultra boards" or
  similar, as a plain statement of fact.

**You may not:**

- use "QuinLED" in the name of your product, project, firmware or board;
- use the QuinLED logo;
- suggest that your product is made, certified or endorsed by QuinLED, unless QuinLED has agreed to that in writing.

Questions: open an issue in this repository.
